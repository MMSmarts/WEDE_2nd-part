Our Services

From routine maintenance to major repairs, our comprehensive auto services have got you covered. We offer specialist care for Land Rover, Range Rover and Jaguar vehicles.

Repairs & Maintenance

Our expert technicians provide oil changes, tyre rotations, engine tune-ups, transmission repairs and more. We will work with you to create a maintenance plan that suits your vehicle and your budget.

Diagnostics

Modern Land Rover and Jaguar vehicles need modern equipment. Our diagnostic tools help us find problems quickly and accurately, so we can fix the right thing the first time.

Mobile Unit

Expert technicians and fully equipped vehicles are available for on-location support at any moment. We can come to you when your vehicle cannot be moved.

Transport & Valet Services

Courtesy vehicles are available for your convenience, so your daily routine is not interrupted. Our valet packages include exterior wash, interior valet, wheel cleaning and tyre dressing.
