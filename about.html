About INNO-MAC JHB

INNO-MAC JHB is an independent Land Rover, Range Rover and Jaguar specialist workshop based in Kya Sands, Randburg, in the north of Johannesburg. We are passionate about keeping your vehicle running smoothly and safely.

Have a look at the services we offer, or book your vehicle in for a service.

Who We Are

We are Land Rover and Range Rover specialists with in-depth knowledge and expertise to keep your vehicle in top shape. From routine maintenance to major repairs, our team has the experience to handle a wide range of services.

We are also an accredited RMI workshop, which means our customers can trust us to work to high industry standards. We use modern diagnostic tools and quality parts to deliver reliable results.

Why Choose Us

  • Proven – Expert Land Rover and Range Rover aftersales specialists and trusted repair and maintenance experts.
  • Accredited – 5-star accredited RMI workshop for expert repair and maintenance services.
  • Diagnostics – Cutting-edge tools and the latest diagnostic technology for accurate fault finding.
  • Mobile Unit – Expert technicians and fully equipped vehicles for on-location support at any moment.
  • Transport Services – Courtesy vehicles available for your convenience.
  • Valet – Packages include exterior wash, interior valet, wheel cleaning and tyre dressing.
